Project Pages: an offline weekly scrapbook app

Upload ALL of these files to the main folder of your GitHub repository (no subfolders):
  index.html, app.js, sw.js, manifest.webmanifest,
  icon-192.png, icon-512.png, icon-maskable.png,
  fredoka-400.woff2, fredoka-600.woff2, sacramento.woff2, playwrite-de-grund-300.woff2,
  FONT-LICENSES.txt, README.txt

Your photos, pages and your Market Fresh and Jonathan Signature fonts are never part of these files.
They are stored only inside the app on your phone. Use "Save project" in the app to back them up.

When you get a new version of the app files later:
  upload the new files over the old ones. The app then shows "A new version is ready" with an Update button.

The bundled fonts (Fredoka, Sacramento, Playwrite DE Grund) are under the SIL Open Font License. See FONT-LICENSES.txt.
